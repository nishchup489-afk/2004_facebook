Getting in touch

If something on Thefacebook is broken, confusing, or just not working the way it should, send a note and say what happened.

The more specific the message is, the faster it can be fixed.

Contact information
General: info@thefacebook.com
Support: support@thefacebook.com
School requests: schools@thefacebook.com
What to include

Include your school, your university email, the page you were using, and what you expected to happen.

Do not send your password. Thefacebook will never need your password to answer a support question.
