Basic agreement

By using Thefacebook, you agree to use the site like a student directory and social network for college communities.

The site works best when accounts represent real students and the information is not used to bother, spam, or impersonate people.

Account rules
Real identity: Use your own university identity and do not create accounts for other people.
One account: A student should only have one active Thefacebook account.
Passwords: Keep your password private. Do not send it through email or contact forms.
Sessions: Logging out clears the current session. Shared computers should always be logged out before leaving.
Site behavior

Do not scrape pages, collect student information in bulk, post fake profile information, or try to access another student's account.

Thefacebook can remove accounts or content that breaks the student directory idea of the project.

Changes

The site is still being built. Features, pages, and rules can change as the project grows.

These terms are written for this project version of Thefacebook, not the modern Facebook product.
